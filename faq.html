Income Tax

Business & personal tax

How do I register for a TIN (Tax Identification Number)?
You can register for a TIN online through the eTax portal. You'll need your national ID or passport. The process is instant and free. Visit any RRA office if you need assistance.
How do I file my tax return online?
Log in to MyRRA portal, go to "Declarations" → "File Return". Select the tax type (Income Tax, VAT, PAYE), fill in the required information, and submit. You'll receive a confirmation receipt.
What payment methods are accepted for taxes?
You can pay taxes via bank transfer (BK, Equity, GTB, etc.), mobile money (MTN, Airtel), or at any RRA office. Generate a payment slip from MyRRA portal before making payment.
How do I register for EBM (Electronic Billing Machine)?
Register on MyRRA portal → Go to "EBM Services" → "Register for EBM". You'll need your TIN, business details, and contact information. RRA will provide training on how to use the EBM system.
How do I declare goods at customs?
Use the Single Window portal to submit customs declarations. You'll need import/export licenses, invoices, packing lists, and transport documents. Contact a clearing agent for assistance.
When is the VAT return deadline?
VAT returns must be filed monthly by the 20th day of the following month. For example, September VAT is due by October 20th. Late filing incurs penalties.
What is the corporate income tax rate in Rwanda?
The standard corporate income tax rate is 30% of taxable profits. Small businesses with turnover below 100 million RWF may qualify for presumptive tax. File annual returns by March 31st.
Is TIN registration free?
Yes, TIN registration is completely free. You can register instantly online through the eTax portal. No documents or fees are required for individual TIN.
What happens if I file my tax return late?
Late filing incurs penalties: 10% of tax due for the first month, plus 5% for each additional month. Interest also applies. File as soon as possible to minimize penalties.
Can I pay taxes in installments?
Yes, you can request a payment plan through MyRRA portal if you're unable to pay the full amount. Submit a written request explaining your situation. RRA will review and may approve installments over 3-12 months.
Do I need an EBM machine for my business?
All VAT-registered businesses must use EBM. If your annual turnover exceeds 20 million RWF, you're required to register for VAT and use EBM. Small businesses can use simplified EBM solutions.
How do I calculate import duties?
Use the Trade Portal to search for your product's HS code and applicable duty rates. Duties range from 0-35% depending on the product. VAT (18%) and excise duty may also apply.
