How to Register a Business

Follow these steps to register your business in Rwanda through RDB's One Stop Centre

1

Reserve Your Business Name

Search and reserve your preferred business name through the RDB online portal or at the One Stop Centre. Name reservation is processed instantly.

Have 2-3 alternative names ready in case your first choice is already taken.
2

Prepare Required Documents

Gather the necessary documents including your National ID or passport, proof of address, and business location details. For companies, prepare articles of association.

National ID / Passport Proof of Address Business Location Map Articles of Association (companies)
3

Submit Registration Application

Submit your application online through the RDB portal or visit the One Stop Centre. You'll receive your Certificate of Registration upon approval.

Apply on RDB Portal
4

Get Your TIN Number

After registration, obtain your Tax Identification Number (TIN) from RRA. This is required for all tax obligations and business transactions.

Look Up Your TIN
5

Register for Taxes

Register your business for applicable taxes on MyRRA portal — including VAT, Corporate Tax, and PAYE for your employees.

Register on MyRRA
6

Get an EBM Machine

Obtain an Electronic Billing Machine from RRA for issuing valid receipts and invoices to your customers. EBM compliance is mandatory for all VAT-registered businesses.

Priority Investment Sectors

Rwanda offers compelling investment opportunities across these high-growth sectors

Agriculture & Agribusiness

Fertile land, favorable climate, and growing demand for processed foods make Rwanda ideal for agricultural investment.

ICT & Innovation

Rwanda is positioning itself as Africa's tech hub with fiber optic coverage, innovation funds, and a young digital-native workforce.

Home to mountain gorillas, national parks, and a growing MICE (Meetings, Incentives, Conferences, Exhibitions) industry.

Types of Business Entities

Choose the right legal structure for your business

Sole Proprietorship

A business owned and operated by one individual. Simple to set up with minimal requirements.

  • One owner, full control
  • Minimal documentation
  • Quick registration
  • Owner personally liable
Most Popular

Limited Liability Company

A separate legal entity that protects shareholders' personal assets. Ideal for growing businesses.

  • 1-50 shareholders
  • Limited liability protection
  • Separate legal entity
  • Easier to raise capital

Branch of Foreign Company

Register a branch of an existing foreign company to operate in Rwanda.

  • Extension of parent company
  • 100% foreign ownership allowed
  • Parent company liable
  • Requires parent docs

Your Tax Obligations After Registration

Once your business is registered, here are your key tax responsibilities with RRA

VAT Returns

File monthly if turnover exceeds RWF 50M, or quarterly if between RWF 20M-50M. Rate: 18%.

Learn about VAT

Corporate Income Tax

Annual tax on business profits. Standard rate: 30%. File within 4 months after year end.

Learn about CIT

PAYE

Withhold tax from employee salaries monthly. Progressive rates from 0% to 30%.

Learn about PAYE

EBM Compliance

Issue EBM receipts for all sales. Submit daily EBM reports to RRA.

EBM Services

NSSF Contributions

Contribute 3% of gross salary for each employee. Employee also contributes 3%.

Visit NSSF

Withholding Tax

15% WHT on payments to non-residents, 2% on certain local services. File monthly.

Learn about WHT
