About Rwanda Revenue Authority

Collecting revenue on behalf of the Government of Rwanda to fund national development since 1999.

Our Mission

To efficiently collect all taxes and other revenues on behalf of the Government of Rwanda, and to provide excellent service to taxpayers while ensuring compliance with tax laws.

Our Vision

To be a world-class revenue administration that is trusted by taxpayers and recognized for integrity, efficiency, and innovation in service delivery.

Our Values

Integrity, professionalism, transparency, accountability, and customer focus guide everything we do. We serve with honesty and respect for every taxpayer.

Our History

Key milestones in RRA's journey

1999

RRA Established

Rwanda Revenue Authority created by Law No. 15/99 to consolidate tax and customs administration under one body.

2005

EBM System Launched

Electronic Billing Machine system introduced to combat VAT fraud and improve tax collection transparency.

2010

MyRRA Portal

Online taxpayer portal launched, enabling e-filing, e-payment and self-service account management.

2015

Trade Portal

Rwanda Trade Portal launched to simplify import/export procedures and boost trade facilitation.

2020

Digital Transformation

Major digital overhaul: mobile tax payments, WhatsApp support, AI-powered chatbot, and paperless processes.

2024

Record Revenue Collection

RRA surpassed RWF 2 trillion in annual revenue collection for the first time, a historic milestone.
